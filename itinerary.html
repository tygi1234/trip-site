🚌 Getting Around ▼

🗼 Paris

  • Navigo Easy card (€2) + 10-trip carnet — easiest for families
  • RER B: CDG Airport ↔ Les Halles (45 min, ~€13/adult)
  • RER A: Paris ↔ Disneyland (35 min, ~€8)
  • Métro: Fast but stairs — buses easier with tired kids
  • Kids under 4 free · Ages 4–9 half price
  • Any metro/RER within Paris = €2.15 flat

🏔️ Switzerland

  • Swiss Travel Pass (6-day): ~CHF 390/adult
  • Kids under 16 ride FREE with Family Card
  • Covers trains, buses, boats + 50% off Jungfraujoch
  • SBB app for live departures
  • Paris → Grindelwald: ~5.5 hrs total
  • Grindelwald → Zurich Airport: ~2.5 hrs
